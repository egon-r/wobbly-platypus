Middle Button Scroll

Effortless scrolling with your middle mouse button. Three modes, one extension.

Download Extension (.zip)

Version 1.0 · Manifest V3 · ~14 KB

Features

🎯

Auto-scroll

Click the middle button and move your mouse. The page scrolls in that direction — faster as you move further from the starting point.

✋

Drag-scroll

Hold the middle button and drag. The page follows your cursor like you're grabbing and pulling the document.

🔄

Click-scroll

Click the middle button once to activate, move your mouse — the page scrolls faster the further you go. Click again to stop. Perfect for long reading sessions.

⚙️

Configurable

Switch between Auto-scroll, Drag-scroll, and Click-scroll instantly from the popup. Your preference syncs across devices via Chrome storage.

⚡

Lightweight

Zero dependencies, minimal permissions (just storage), smooth requestAnimationFrame-powered scrolling.

🔓

Open

Fully open source. Inspect every line of code in the unzipped extension folder before installing. No secrets, no surprises.

Installation

  1. Download the extension.zip file above.
  2. Unzip the archive anywhere on your computer.
  3. Open chrome://extensions/ in Chrome.
  4. Enable Developer mode (toggle top-right).
  5. Click Load unpacked and select the extension/ folder.
  6. Done! Click the extension icon to configure your scroll mode.
